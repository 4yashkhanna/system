T: NORTH STAR
T: To enable neighbourhoods and festivals to adapt to each other while maintaining a balance between celebration and everyday city life.
T: WHERE TO ACT (★)
T: 1. Give private adjustment a voice, so changes made by residents, shops and delivery workers reach planners (#7, #23, #35, #82). 2. Make site choice a yearly decision that weighs access and traffic, not only carry-over (#17, #28, #62, #84). 3. One plan for the whole cycle, setup to clean-up, shared by police and PMC (#19, #79, #86). 4. Plan teardown crews and dates together with setup (#19, #53, #65). 5. Record lessons each year so learning does not reset with new staff (#3, #30, #83). 6. Size the procession to capacity before the day, with clear rules for who joins (#63). 7. Put up signs as well as volunteers (#39, #61). 8. Keep a community role in rituals alongside civic services (#80). 9. A kept-clear emergency corridor near big pandals, with response time measured (#38, #85).
