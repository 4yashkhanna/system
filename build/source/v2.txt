[665:8388] NORTH STAR To enable neighbourhoods and festivals to adapt to each other while maintaining a balance between celebration and everyday city life.
T: WHAT THE DEEPER MAP ADDS
T: The festival side now has six engines that grow it: prestige (R1), attention (R2), belonging (R3), patronage (R4), the marketplace (R5) and site lock-in (R6). Almost every brake runs through one route: harm to residents becomes complaints, complaints and media become rules, and rules arrive a season late (B2, B3, B5, B6). Residents who adapt quietly (R9) and enforcement that bends to patronage, VIP visits and permission load keep those brakes weak. That is the gap between this map and the North Star.

T: WHERE TO ACT (WEAKEST TO STRONGEST, AFTER MEADOWS)
T: Numbers: more barricades and police (B1, B12) help on the night but push congestion onto other roads (R7). Delays: rules land a year after the harm (B2, B3, B6). An in-season review would let the brakes work sooner. Information: give private adaptation a channel to planners (R9) and send advance notice (B7). Cheap, and it serves near stars 2 and 6. Rules: make the site a yearly decision instead of a carry-over (R6), and treat enforcement as the hinge it is. Goals and mindsets: "every year it should be bigger" and "the festival comes first" drive R1 to R3. Shared responsibility between organisers, communities and authorities is the alternative goal.
