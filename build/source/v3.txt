T: SYSTEM MAP v3: THE ICEBERG LOOPS, MERGED
T: We took every ★ loop on the iceberg (the ones that move us toward the North Star) and the loops that connect them. Each loop stays a visible ring; where two or more loops run through the same variable, their rings are merged at one shared box. Built from the 86 loop sections on this board.
[818:9032] NORTH STAR To enable neighbourhoods and festivals to adapt to each other while maintaining a balance between celebration and everyday city life.
T: HOW TO READ IT Each coloured ring is one loop from the iceberg. Orange circle = reinforcing (R), blue circle = balancing (B); the circle names the iceberg loop numbers. Dark box ◆ = MERGE POINT: a variable that two or more loops share, so their rings are joined there. Outlined grey box ◇ = LINK POINT: loops connect here through a dashed grey arrow (an outside influence in one loop that is a variable in another). Violet arrow = one arrow shared by two loops. Yellow outline + ★ = where acting moves us toward the North Star. Arrow labels: + same direction, − opposite direction; E evidence, H hypothesis, ? sign not known.

T: HOW THE LOOPS MERGE 1. Silent adaptation. #7/#82 (residents) and #23/#56 (errands, deliveries) run through one merge point: private route, timing and trip changes. The more people adjust quietly, the less disruption reaches authorities, so arrangements do not change. #69 (complaints) and #83 (familiarity) feed the same point through links. 2. Closures. #84 (site lock-in), #14/#60 (barricades) and #20/#32 (diversions) all pass through closures, barricades and diversions. From there, links reach errands (#23), advisories (#35, #3), volunteer signage (#39), emergency access (#85) and the procession (#63). 3. Site precedent. #28 (tradition) and #62 (layout reuse) both harden carry-over permissions, which also feed the main-road choice (#84) and the chowki relationship (#59/#71). 4. Coordination. The balancing loops of #29 and #59/#71 share two merge points: clashes on the ground and the gap to one agreed plan. The single window and coordination both feed #79, one plan for the whole cycle. 5. Borrowed capacity. #16/#37/#73 (police at hotspots) and #81 (capacity reallocated) share three merge points. Stretched services slow the #19/#65/#86 clean-up, and #53 crews push teardown later. 6. Learning. #7 and #30 share changes to arrangements. Problems only feed #30 when they are visible, and #82 and #83 keep them out of sight.
